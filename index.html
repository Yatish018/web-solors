Common questions

Will solar work on cloudy days?

Yes, at reduced output. Rajasthan gets strong sun most of the year, so annual generation is high.

Is there a government subsidy?

Homes can get a subsidy under the PM Surya Ghar scheme. Amounts change, so we confirm the current figure in your quote.

How long do panels last?

Good panels are usually warranted for 25 years, with inverter and installation warranties on top.
